SecPrep is built entirely for cybersecurity. Every question, domain, and piece of feedback is written by security professionals. How does the Elite job matching work?+
Our AI scans thousands of cybersecurity job postings daily across LinkedIn, company career pages, and niche security job boards. It matches listings to your role and experience, then generates a tailored resume and cover letter for each one. You review, customize if needed, and apply.

Yes. Our question bank is built from hundreds of interviews reported by real candidates at top security companies. We also work with hiring managers to validate that questions reflect what's actually asked in technical screens at each experience level.
