Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

At SecPrep, your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. We've written it in plain language so you can actually understand it.

Who We Are

SecPrep ("we", "us", "our") operates the SecPrep platform at secprep.vercel.app. We provide AI-powered cybersecurity interview preparation services. For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@secprep.io.

What Data We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you register
  • Interview data — questions attempted, answers submitted, scores, and session history
  • Payment information — billing details processed securely by Stripe (we never store your card details)
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction patterns
  • Device data — browser type, operating system, IP address, and general location
  • Communications — emails or messages you send us

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, biometrics, or health information.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the SecPrep platform
  • Personalize your interview sessions and track your progress
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send you account-related notifications and product updates
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our question bank and AI coaching
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

AI Processing

Your interview answers are processed by Google's Gemini AI model to generate feedback and scores. By using SecPrep, you acknowledge that your responses are transmitted to Google's API for processing. Google's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy. We do not use your interview answers to train our own models.

💡 In plain English: When you type an answer, it gets sent to Google's AI to generate feedback. Google processes it according to their own privacy policy. We don't store your answers long-term or use them to train AI models.

Data Sharing

We share your data only with trusted service providers who help us operate SecPrep:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI coaching and feedback generation
  • Vercel — hosting and infrastructure
  • Email providers — transactional and marketing emails

All third-party providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify. We may also disclose your data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes. Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how people use SecPrep. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the Service.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing of your data
  • Withdrawal — withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@secprep.io. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure payment processing via Stripe, and access controls on our systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

SecPrep is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

International Transfers

SecPrep operates in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US. By using SecPrep, you consent to this transfer. We take steps to ensure adequate protections are in place for international data transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on the Service. Your continued use of SecPrep after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please contact us at privacy@secprep.io. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.
